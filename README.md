## 👋 About Me

I am **Samuel Oliver**, a technology leader and software engineer with **8+ years of experience** across software engineering, DevOps, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, governance, risk and compliance, and technology leadership.

I currently lead **Cura ID**, a **global health identity platform**, where I work across engineering, infrastructure, security and product strategy to build systems that improve trust, accessibility and resilience in digital healthcare.

My work sits at the intersection of **technology and organisational strategy**. I enjoy taking complex technical problems, turning them into practical systems, and building the engineering and governance structures required to operate those systems reliably at scale.

### What I Bring

* **Engineering depth** with hands-on full-stack development experience
* **Cloud and DevOps expertise** across modern infrastructure and deployment environments
* **Security and GRC knowledge** across frameworks, controls and enterprise risk
* **Technology leadership** with experience translating business requirements into technical strategy
* **Systems thinking** across architecture, integration, automation and operational resilience
* **A strong interest in emerging technologies**, particularly digital identity, AI, blockchain and healthcare technology
